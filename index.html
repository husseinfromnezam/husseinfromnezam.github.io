Property management · Toronto2026 · built and run

AI phone receptionists on two lines, tuned against a score on every call

ProblemCalls went unanswered, all of them after hours and some during the day. The ones that were answered often never reached the CRM, so leads died in a voicemail box.

BuildTwo voice agents on Retell AI with n8n behind them. Each call is answered in seconds; the agent qualifies the caller, takes the property and contact details, and posts a structured summary to Slack, tagging the right person. Every call is then pulled, scored by Gemini against a written rubric, stored in Supabase and shown on a dashboard the owner opens. That loop drove 57 agent versions.

Manufacturing platform · EgyptJun 2025 to Jan 2026 · in-house

An ERP from scratch, and instant quoting from a CAD file

ProblemAn on-demand B2B manufacturing platform routing parts to partner factories, run on spreadsheets. Quotes were built by hand in Word with a Gantt chart pasted from Excel, two hours of the founder's day. Purchase orders, supplier payments and the CRM lived in files that disagreed with each other.

BuildSpent the first weeks watching every process, then built an ERP specific to the business: orders tracked from quote to factory to delivery, supplier balances, a CRM automated in ClickUp and integrated into the ERP. Quoting became a pipeline: Python and CadQuery read the STEP file (bounding box, volume, surface area, bosses, holes), n8n assembles the priced PDF, and a Digifabster integration fed a 7,000-point dataset for an in-house pricing model. An AI layer on top lets leadership ask the business questions in plain language.

      03 · Method

      How I work

      Business value first, then the process, then the data, and a number agreed before a line of code. A tool nobody adopts is worth nothing, so the people doing the work are in the room from day one.

      01

      Watch, then ask

      A week or two beside the team. Two questions to everyone: what wastes most of your time, and what should you be doing instead? Leadership gets the same questions separately; the answers rarely match, and both count.

      rule #1
copy-paste is not part of anyone's workflow
      02

      Pull the data

      CRM exports, call logs, inbox volumes, the spreadsheets themselves. There are always hints. The receptionist project began when the call log showed a third of calls arriving after hours and none answered.

      baseline-2026-05-13.json
"captured_at": "2026-05-13T15:33:18Z"
"llms_txt": 404 · "crawler": 403
      03

      Cost it, rank it, agree the number

      Manual cost today against build, maintenance and API cost. The gap has to be smaller than the value. Then the owner and I agree what success looks like as a number, and what the dashboard will show.

      target      contact capture ≥ 80%
baseline    after-hours answered 0%
report      weekly, plain numbers
      04

      Build, measure, keep the record

      Ship the highest-value item that can go live in weeks. Every automation gets its own metric and a status log that stands alone, so the next person (or the next session) knows exactly where things are.

      STATUS.md   newest entry on top
HANDOFF.md  last write of a session
ledger      claim · check · source · time
